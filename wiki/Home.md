restaurant webapp concept created from the Wayne Tech Lab S.F.W.A. Template /

## Product Direction

**Brand:** Bardo's Breakfast Burgers  
**Location concept:** Salem, Oregon  
**Core offer:** Breakfast sandwiches, smash burgers, plates, pickup, catering,
and rewards  
**Technical base:** React, TypeScript, Vite, Firebase-ready configuration, and
SYSTEMX operator tooling

## Current Webapp Scope

- Home page with Bardo's logo, Salem positioning, and pickup/menu calls to action.
- About page with local restaurant direction and brand feel.
- Menu page with starter breakfast, burger, and catering items.
- Contact page for future ordering, catering, and customer messages.
- Rewards route using the inherited demo account-level system.
- Documentation pages retained for future Firebase, CI, QA, and deployment work.

## Production Notes

Before a live launch, verify restaurant address, operating hours, menu prices,
food imagery, payment provider, privacy language, accessibility, analytics,
ordering integrations, and support process.
